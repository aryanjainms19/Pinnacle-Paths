# Pinnacle Paths: Extended Feature Blueprint

Here are the suggested features built on top of your framework, specifically tailored for a GenZ target audience and their DIY travel spirit:

### 1. "Vibe Check" Filters for Treks
Instead of just filtering by location, let users filter by the *vibe*. Categories could include:
*   "Digital Detox" (no network zones)
*   "Aesthetic Cafes" (great for trails near places like Jibhi or Nainital)
*   "Hardcore Summit" 
*   "Weekend Getaway"

### 2. Offline Survival Mode
GenZ hikers know the internet drops the second you gain altitude. Allow users to hit a "Download Itinerary" button that saves their day-by-day plan, cafe list, and trail map locally on their phone for offline access.

### 3. "Fit Check & Gear" Pack List Generator
Since this is for independent planners, provide an automated checklist based on the selected trek's current weather. It can suggest essentials (like microspikes or a power bank) alongside fun additions (like disposable cameras or UNO cards for the homestay).

### 4. Spotify / Apple Music Integration
Add a "Trail Tunes" section to the itinerary. Users can connect their Spotify, or the app can recommend community-curated playlists tailored to the specific vibe of the trek (e.g., "Acoustic Sunrise" for the final summit push).

### 5. AI "Leave No Trace" Bounty
Tie this into your AI image verification for medals. If users upload photos showing they picked up trash on the trail or at the summit, the AI recognizes the trash bag/cleanup and awards them a special "Eco-Warrior" badge or gives them discounts at partner cafes in the local market.

### 6. Split The Bill / Squad Link
If they are planning a trip with a group, allow them to invite friends to the itinerary via a unique link. Include a very simple, built-in expense tracker so they don't have to jump between Splitwise and your app to figure out who paid for the cafe or the homestay.
